## Welcome to Doc Fletcher

“When life gives you lemons, you make lemonade.”

We’re not sure what happened but one day I woke up and I had a great website and then the next day, I didn’t. We lost most of the old website too, sorry folks. The good news is that I’ve finally decided to expand my website and make it something even better then it was before!

So stay tuned gang for something even greater.

When I was two years old, my Dad was stationed with the Army Air Corps in California. My mom’s cousin, Johnny Perfetta, sang with Kaye Starr in Joe Venutti’s Big Band. One day we went to Kaye’s house for a party and Cecil B. DeMille was there (true story) he thought I was cute, so he offered my Mom a part for me in his upcoming film. Mom turned it down, because she hadn’t seen her family for two years. I have been trying to get that offer again ever since! Anyway, since then, I have traveled across this great country as a saloon singer with two of my five brothers and one of my two sisters. I have done lots of commercials and films. Not as a big star though. And I adore the many shows I’ve done on stage.

From time to time I’ve tried to get out of show business. I managed an upper-class country club, owned my own tavern, did children’s photography, taught school. And a bunch of other stuff I’d really rather forget. But, just like The Godfather: ” Every time I try to get out, they pull me back in.”

I consider myself very lucky to be an actor, singer, and all-around performer. (I’ll do anything to keep from getting a real job.) What the heck, I make a living. I love my life!

But, if anybody’s listening “I’m ready for my close up Mr. DeMille !!!!”

## The FAQs

### Q1: How did you get the name Doc?
Answer: Contrary to some of my card playing partners, it has nothing to do with
my abilities at Poker. In actuality, when I was in the seventh grade I had an absent-minded music  teacher, who couldn’t remember his students names. So, over the years he developed a seating chart that utilized the seven dwarfs in the rhythm section and the eight Reindeer in the horn section, etc. I just happened to be in the seat where all of the previous Doc’s had sat. I was the only one to whom the nickname stuck.

### Q2: What is your real job?
I don’t know why the public in general has such a hard time believing that, just because you are not a big star, you can’t be as successful in the arts as most people are at their chosen careers. I deal with it now and I dealt with it, when I was a musician. But, believe me it can be done.

### Q3: If your Sicilian, how did you get a name like Fletcher?

Well, I usually say I’m Sicilian or Italian, because I was raised in a neighborhood that was primarily Italian, with the Italian side of my
family. My grandmother and Grandfather, Uncle Joey, Uncle Fred, and Aunt Josephine all lived within five houses of me. The Irish side, my Father’s
side, were in a different State and I rarely got to see them. And to be truthful, they didn’t identify with their European heritage so much.

### Q4: I see another comic/actor around NY named Clayton Fletcher. Is he your son or (since you look so very, very young) brother?

He’s my nephew. You can easily see the resemblance. He’s over six feet tall and I’m a little more than a little under that.

### Q5: Are you related to the nationally known jazz bassist and piano player - Ashton Fletcher , who has performed with such notables as Frank Sinatra, Elvis Presley, Bobby Vinton, Carmen MacRae, and Barney Kessel?

I am proud to say that he is my younger brother, but not that much younger

### Q6: Is your Mom the original Mother Fletcher that Jackie Gleason used to talk about?

Of course!

### Q7: Is it true that nobody in your family has ever been convicted of a felony?

Why do you ask?

### Q8: Are there really two “Shrinks” who specialize in treating the rest of you in your family?

Yes, but we never pay them for it and Mom says that they are just as crazy as the rest of us.

### Q9: Would you mind closing your eyes, spreading your arms tilting your head back, and touching your nose with your fingertips?

But officer, I’m the designated driver.
